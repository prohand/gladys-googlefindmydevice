# Changelog

All notable changes to this integration are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses
[semantic versioning](https://semver.org/), bumped by the Release workflow.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Google Find My Device (Find Hub) devices in Gladys: Android phones and tablets and Find Hub
  network trackers, from the `secrets.json` generated by GoogleFindMyTools.
- Features per device: location, at home, distance from home, position accuracy and a Ring switch.
- End-to-end encrypted locations decrypted locally (own and crowd-sourced reports).
- Fresh positions pushed by Google (FCM channel), automatic locate every 15 minutes (configurable,
  or on demand only).
- Scene triggers: a device has a new position, arrives home, leaves home.
- Scene actions: locate a device (returns the position), ring a device, stop a device ringing.
- "Find My Device" dashboard widget: every device or a single one, with Locate and Ring buttons.
- Configuration buttons: test the connection, locate a device, ring a device.
- English, French, German and Spanish texts and documentation.
